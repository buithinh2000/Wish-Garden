# Wish Garden — Product Philosophy & Business Model
**Canonical strategy v1 · 6 November 2026 · Owner direction**

## North star
Wish Garden is a **universal, nonreligious Wishing Tree community** where people stand beneath a luminous tree, offer their intentions, and experience symbolic resonance with other people and the cosmos. The tree is a place of reflection, not a deity, temple, church, or claim of supernatural powers. English-first, multilingual and accessible to Eastern and Western cultures.

**Core principle: hope is free. Revenue comes from hosting, customizing and curating spaces—not selling spiritual outcomes or prioritizing human suffering.**

## Experience design: emotional, not gamified
1. Arrive at the main tree; ambient visitors can make the place feel inhabited. A clear label states **simulated background visitors**. Never fabricate a real member count, real identities, or user wishes.
2. Approach or tap the tree. Light gently travels among its branches; the avatar may stand quietly at the tree. Touching is optional, never an obstacle to wishing.
3. Write an intention *within the world*, choose save-only-on-device or do-not-save. No religious language, guaranteed cosmic acceptance, automated wish disclosure, or paid access to wishing.
4. Release. A unique light rises from the tree into the sky and, with consent, joins a **future collective dispatch queue** once authenticated service exists. The current release is **symbolic only**.
5. Return to revisit private wishes, backup, or attend the next collective event. Show an honest schedule, queue state, and proof only where backed by actual external receipts.

### Emotional community without deceptive social proof
- **MVP:** 3–6 ambient silhouette visitors rendered locally and plainly identified as **illustrative / simulated**, not counted as online people. They do not publish fabricated user messages or react as real people.
- **When backend and moderation ship:** separately display real opt-in visitors and verified counts, privacy-preserving public wish lights. Never infer who is present by exposing private device-only wishes.
- Low-motion, no sound by default, mobile controls minimal, no religious objects or costumes.

## Real dispatch: state machine (not marketing fiction)
- **Draft** → **Opted in** → **Queued** → **Partner accepted** → **Transmitted (verified)** or **Failed / postponed / cancelled**.
- A wish can remain private in My Sky **without** consent to external transmission. Radio broadcasts cannot be recalled; explicit separate opt-in is required, and any broadcast package must remove sensitive content and enforce length/content constraints.
- Use clearly labelled **symbolic sending** until a real provider gives operator identity, channel, quota, actual antenna path, timestamp and auditable evidence. A site claim that an antenna transmits is *not* evidence of Wish Garden's transmitted package.
- Do **not** sell "better cosmic reception", "more likely granted", scientific resonance or guaranteed results.
- Calendar countdown only after confirmed dates; otherwise mark *planned / date to be announced*.

## Business model (hypotheses, not committed prices)
**Always free:** visit main Wishing Tree, personal symbolic wish, private device memories, export/delete, privacy controls and participation in confirmed public community events within fair-use limits.

Potential monetization after validated demand:
1. **Hosted Trees:** personal/family/community hosts pay for a dedicated tree with a custom name, controlled invitations and theme. Candidate tiers to test: Free (basic hosted preview with limitations), Personal **A$5–9/month**, Community **A$19–39/month**. No paywall on basic wishing at the public tree.
2. **Tree Design Packs:** one-time **A$3–15** for bespoke scenery/seasonal decor/lighting; strictly aesthetic and optional. Prefer free first-party/CC0 packs for MVP.
3. **Events & host services:** community anniversaries, charity groups, schools, memorial/gathering hosts, **A$49–149 per event** for private scheduling and custom ritual production. Strict content moderation and host accountability.
4. **Organizations/B2B:** wellness/community organization white-label spaces, consented attendance insight only, never exploit private intentions, employer surveillance or targeted advertising.
5. **Optional physical/commemorative keepsakes** only if demand validates; no claims of perpetual storage.

Illustrative unit economics (AUD, not forecast): 100 paid hosts × A$9/mo = **A$900 MRR gross** before platform fees, hosting, support, tax and churn. Break-even requires verified infrastructure costs and conversion; never present as expected revenue.

**North-star metric:** weekly returning people who voluntarily complete a reflective ritual and report a sense of connection, not ritual clicks or time spent compulsively. Supporting metrics: first-wish completion, 7/30-day opt-in returns, private-data deletion/restore success, consent rates, host retention, verified event proof and uptime.

### Guardrails
- Distinguish avatars from real humans and real totals.
- No ads beside vulnerable user-generated wishes, no paid ranking of emotional need.
- No collection of minors' private or sensitive confessions without safeguards and informed consent. No pressure, fear-based or miracle claims.
- Every public tree needs content moderation, reporting, privacy and host responsibilities before user-generated messages go public.
- No new paid infrastructure or billing deployment without explicit Owner approval.
- Product name & promise remain **Wish Garden: Wishing Tree / Collective Hope / Messages to the Universe**.
