# Wish Garden — Zero-cost options for a REAL space signal
Date: 2026-11-05
Status: research only; no user messages submitted, no service integrated, no payment.

## New non-negotiable constraints
- **No paid provider** and no purchased ground station or satellite hardware.
- A meaningful physical event (actual radio emission above Earth, a satellite relay, or a physical item flown aboard spacecraft) is required. A server submission, star animation, distance counter, or blockchain transaction does not meet that definition.
- Proof of a request or boarding pass must not be described as proof of completed transmission or launch.
- Never automatically send the private wish, name, or email to another service; separate explicit consent and third-party privacy disclosure required.

## Ranked options (2026-11-05)
### 1. NASA Dragonfly Journey to Titan — free, authoritative; *physical launch is future*
NASA announcement (2026-10-29): https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/send-your-name-to-titan-aboard-nasas-dragonfly-mission/
Signup: https://go.nasa.gov/dragonfly-names
The public may submit **a name**, receive a digital boarding pass, and have the name included on a physical microchip scheduled to fly aboard the Dragonfly mission. Signups close **15 January 2027, 11:59pm EST**. Launch currently planned in 2028, arrival Titan in 2034. It is a genuine planned physical payload, NOT a radio transmission and NOT yet in space. Do not submit on another person's behalf or misrepresent a boarding pass as proof of flight.

### 2. SpaceSpeak — free text submission, radio transmission claimed; *verification required*
https://www.spacespeak.com/Learn/Messages
https://spacespeak.com/Learn/FAQ
SpaceSpeak states **free text up to 2,000 characters**, radio transmitter of about **2–5 GHz** with 18-inch dish, a queued status page and a launch date after transmission. We have not independently verified site operations, current availability, schedule, RF logs, frequency licensing, station identifier, third-party receiving confirmation, API, or private-message visibility. Never promise that submissions have been broadcast without real independent proof.

### 3. gateto.space — one free 32-character radio transmission claimed; beta gated
https://gateto.space/
Provider claims one introductory **32-character** transmission from its ground station per account, with more messages earned by in-game points. It also claims an RF emission, but invites beta accounts in waves. No external proof of the antenna, transmission scheduling, licensing or receipt has been independently validated yet. Not a direct immediate backend solution for Wish Garden.

### 4. CosmoPost — zero cost, insufficient independent physical evidence
https://cosmopost.space/
The website claims no-registration free RF emission from a Yagi antenna; it also explicitly describes its distance display as a **simulation** using light speed after an alleged transmission. Published descriptions alone are not independent evidence that a given user's message was ever radiated. Do not integrate before audited station details and receipts.

## Product choice
- Keep Wish Garden V0.2Q's star release 100% free, symbolic and private by default.
- Optional informational *Space Journey* exploration can link to NASA's official no-fee campaign, plainly labelled "Name registered for a future mission"; **do not add a sent-to-space badge until NASA confirms the actual spacecraft launched**.
- For direct RF, conduct one consented test with a harmless public project phrase (e.g. `WISH GARDEN PEACE FOR ALL`), via a provider's own UI. Request station identity, transmission UTC timestamp, actual RF waveform/log reference, and ideally a third-party independent SDR reception. No proof → no in-app “sent to space” claim.
- Even if a service is free for an individual, free usage might be rate-limited or prohibit embedding/commercial integration. Website-to-provider integration is NOT guaranteed.
- No uplinks, subscriptions, keys, identity data transfer, NASA registration or project user wishes initiated in this research step.
