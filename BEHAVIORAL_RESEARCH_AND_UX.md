# Wish Garden — Behavioral Research and Ethical UX (2026-11-06)

## Design goal
People visit the Wishing Tree to feel connected to themselves, to other humans, and symbolically to the universe. **Never claim the universe has read a wish or that an AI can predict its outcome.** No religion-specific shrine, incense, bell, ritual hierarchy or paid miracles.

## Evidence and translations to UX

| Insight / scientific limitation | Source | Wish Garden decision |
|---|---|---|
| Rituals have theorized emotion, goal and social regulation functions. But effects depend on meaning/context, and authors caution about ritual dependence. | Hobson et al., 2018, *Psychology of Rituals* https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29130838/ | Small 2–3 gesture ritual: stand at tree, optionally reflect, release light. No repetition quota, scarcity, fear or streak penalties. |
| Five studies found awe can increase helping/prosocial measures; one involved standing under tall trees. Not proof of mental-health treatment. | Piff et al., 2015 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25984788/ | Tree should be visually central and convey scale, gentle warmth, stars, connected lights. No claims of clinical efficacy. |
| Meta-analysis of 21 studies, 15,907 participants found small-to-moderate benefits for mental contrasting with implementation intentions (g≈0.336), with publication-bias cautions. | Wang et al., 2021 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8149892/ | Optional reflective questions and, later, a voluntary small personal step. Do not prescribe a future outcome or pressure users. |
| Kau Cim's culturally specific ritual draws a single numbered stick and reads a poem/interpretation. | HKU cultural entry https://learning.hku.hk/ccch9051/group-20/items/show/43 | Borrow only one-question/one-card/pause structure, **not divination, deity, lucky ratings, celestial predictions or copied ritual names**. A 'Reflection Leaf' is an authored introspective question, explicitly not fortune-telling. |
| Humanlike virtual agents can increase social evaluation and alter disclosure of sensitive answers, even if they feel warm. | 2025 randomized study https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S074756322500130X | Prefer stylized non-speaking ambient companions; no fake avatars pretending to be real community or probing personal wishes. Clearly label simulated population. |
| Preregistered experimental study did **not** find direct anxiety-suppression support for ritual actions in one sample. | Karl et al., 2018 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30306399/ | Do not claim rituals lower anxiety for everyone or market as treatment. |

## Current MVP behaviors (zero backend)
- A central luminous Wishing Tree responds to a user's voluntary touch and wish-release light.
- Four lightweight, motionless, neutral-garbed **illustrative human silhouettes**, deliberately labeled as simulated and **not real visitor counts**.
- A voluntary **Reflection Leaf** inside the scene, chosen from a short fixed set of original, nonpredictive questions. The question is not stored, uploaded or used to score people; user may skip entirely.
- Existing private wish, save-on-device choice, memory/export/delete, accessible mobile flow, and truthful symbolic-release label preserved.
- No promotions, paid gates, growth nudges, fake testimonials or manipulative retention loops.

## Retention hypotheses to test with consenting users, not claims
1. More people finish their first ceremony when the tree stays visible and three gestures are coherent (visit, reflect if wanted, release).
2. Explicitly simulated silhouettes help a few people feel less alone without deceit, but others may prefer empty space; **add an accessibility/visitor toggle** when validated.
3. A one-question Reflection Leaf may increase sense of meaning; a nonparticipant control is necessary to evaluate benefit rather than assuming it.
4. The honest planned-universe event schedule may create return interest only when actual transmission evidence exists.

## Research / consent before community launch
Conduct 5–8 moderated usability sessions across language and cultural contexts; include mobile, low-end device, reduced motion, hearing impairment. Ask: Where would you stand? Did you know avatars were simulated? Was privacy clear? Did you think the universe literally received your message? Would you return without reminders? Stop if content causes distress; never harvest sensitive confessions as test data.

**Success metric:** user-reported sense of calm/meaning, completion without confusion, voluntarily returning after 7 days, correct understanding of privacy and symbolic versus actual dispatch. **Anti-metrics:** coerced repeats, fear of negative outcomes, fake presence, revelation of private wishes, perceived religious affiliation.

## Commercial implication
Free shared tree, free wishing/reflection/private memory. Later paid hosted trees, decoration, community events; never better "cosmic luck" for money. The detailed commercialization hypotheses and safety gates are in PRODUCT_AND_BUSINESS_MODEL.md.
